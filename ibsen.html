Hana Tolera

The Wild Duck

Act 4

Scene

Hjalmar

Light the lamp!

Gina

(lighting it)

And then we don’t know that it is Mr. Werle himself; it may be Graberg who——

Hjalmar

Why these subterfuges about Graberg?

Gina

I don’t know, I only thought——

Hjalmar

H’m!

Gina

Well, it wasn’t me that got grandfather the writing. Why it was Bertha when she went to the house.

Hjalmar

Your voice seems to me to be trembling.
